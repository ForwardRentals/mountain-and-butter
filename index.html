The idea

Good food tastes better outside.

Mountain & Butter is about food that fits a mountain life: something warm before a dawn start, something sturdy in your pack, and something golden from the oven when you get home cold and tired.

Expect real butter, cast iron, seasonal fruit and recipes you can make in a small kitchen, a cabin or at a campsite.
