Sheet-Pan Lemon Dill Salmon

Wild salmon roasted with lemon, onion, cherry tomatoes and fresh dill. Dinner in 20 minutes, one pan to wash.

Prep10 min
Cook12–15 min
MakesServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb (680 g) salmon fillet, cut into 4 pieces
  • 1 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • 2 tbsp butter, melted, or olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, grated
  • Small bunch fresh dill
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds (optional)
  • Salt and freshly ground pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Scatter the onion and tomatoes over the pan, toss with half the butter and a pinch of salt, and roast for 5 minutes while you prep the fish.
  3. Mix the rest of the butter with the garlic. Nestle the salmon among the vegetables, brush with the garlic butter and season well with salt, pepper and sesame seeds.
  4. Tuck the lemon wedges around the fish and roast 10–12 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily but is still a little translucent in the centre.
  5. Scatter with fresh dill and squeeze the roasted lemon over everything before serving.
Kitchen notePull the salmon a minute early. It keeps cooking on the hot pan, and wild salmon dries out fast.
