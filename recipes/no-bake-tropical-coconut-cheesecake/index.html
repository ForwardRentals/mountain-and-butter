No-Bake Tropical Coconut Cheesecake

A creamy cashew and coconut cheesecake on a date-nut crust, piled with mango, kiwi, passion fruit and toasted coconut.

Prep40 min
CookNo bake
MakesOne 7-inch cake

Ingredients

Crust

  • 1 cup (120 g) raw almonds or walnuts
  • 1 cup (150 g) soft Medjool dates, pitted
  • 2 tbsp shredded coconut
  • Pinch of salt

Filling

  • 2 cups (280 g) raw cashews, soaked 4 hours or overnight
  • ¾ cup (180 ml) full-fat coconut milk
  • ⅓ cup (80 ml) maple syrup
  • ⅓ cup (75 g) coconut oil, melted
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • ½ cup (80 g) ripe mango, for a mango layer (optional)

Topping

  • 1 mango, diced
  • 2 kiwi, sliced
  • 2 passion fruit, halved
  • Coconut flakes and goji berries

Method

  1. Line the base of a 7-inch springform pan with parchment. Pulse the nuts, dates, coconut and salt in a food processor until the mix sticks together when pressed. Press firmly into the pan and chill.
  2. Drain and rinse the cashews. Blend them with the coconut milk, maple syrup, coconut oil, lemon juice and vanilla until completely smooth, scraping down a few times. This can take 3–5 minutes.
  3. For a two-tone cake, pour two-thirds of the filling over the crust. Blend the mango into the rest and pour it on top.
  4. Cover and freeze for at least 6 hours, or overnight, until firm.
  5. Move to the fridge 30–60 minutes before serving. Top with mango, kiwi, passion fruit pulp, coconut flakes and goji berries just before it goes to the table.
Kitchen noteSlice with a knife dipped in hot water and wiped dry between cuts for clean edges.
