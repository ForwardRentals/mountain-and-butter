Brown Butter Trail Cookies

Chewy oat cookies with nutty brown butter, dark chocolate and a pinch of flaky salt. Built to survive a backpack.

Prep20 min
Cook12 min
Makes18 cookies

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup (170 g) unsalted butter
  • 1 cup (200 g) packed brown sugar
  • ¼ cup (50 g) white sugar
  • 1 large egg + 1 yolk
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 1 ¼ cups (160 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup (90 g) rolled oats
  • ¾ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp fine salt
  • 150 g dark chocolate, chopped
  • Flaky salt, for the tops

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a light-coloured pan over medium heat, swirling, until it foams, smells nutty and the bits on the bottom turn golden brown, about 5 minutes. Pour into a large bowl and let cool for 10 minutes.
  2. Whisk both sugars into the butter, then the egg, yolk and vanilla until glossy.
  3. Stir in the flour, oats, baking soda and salt until just combined, then fold in the chocolate. Chill the dough for at least 20 minutes (or overnight).
  4. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Scoop 2-tablespoon balls onto a lined sheet, spaced well apart, and sprinkle with flaky salt.
  5. Bake 10–12 minutes, until the edges are set but the centres still look soft. Cool on the sheet for 5 minutes before moving.
Kitchen noteWrap two cookies back to back in parchment. They stay soft for days and don't crumble in your pack.
