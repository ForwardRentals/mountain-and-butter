One-Pan Cast-Iron Camp Breakfast

Crispy potatoes, peppers and eggs cooked in a single skillet over the fire or camp stove. One pan to wash.

Prep10 min
Cook25 min
MakesServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp oil
  • 4 small potatoes, diced small
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper
  • 4–6 eggs
  • Handful of grated cheddar
  • Chopped green onion and hot sauce, to serve

Method

  1. Heat the butter and oil in a 10–12 inch cast-iron skillet over a medium camp stove or a grate above glowing coals (not flames).
  2. Add the potatoes in one layer with a pinch of salt. Cook 12–15 minutes, turning every few minutes, until golden and tender. Cover with a lid or foil for part of the time to speed things up.
  3. Add the onion, pepper and paprika and cook 5 minutes more, until soft.
  4. Make wells in the hash, crack in the eggs, scatter the cheese over everything and cover. Cook 4–6 minutes, until the whites are set.
  5. Finish with green onion, pepper and hot sauce. Eat straight from the pan.
Kitchen noteDice the potatoes at home and keep them in water in a sealed container in the cooler. Pat dry before they hit the pan.
