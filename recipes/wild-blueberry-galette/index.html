Wild Blueberry Butter Galette

A rustic, all-butter pastry folded around late-summer blueberries. No pie dish and no fuss.

Prep30 min
Cook45 min
MakesServes 6

Ingredients

Pastry

  • 1 ¼ cups (160 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ cup (113 g) cold butter, cubed
  • 3–5 tbsp ice water

Filling

  • 3 cups (450 g) blueberries, wild if you can find them
  • ⅓ cup (65 g) sugar
  • 1 ½ tbsp cornstarch
  • Zest and juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 egg, beaten, plus coarse sugar for the crust

Method

  1. Mix the flour, sugar and salt. Rub in the cold butter with your fingertips until you have pea-sized pieces, then add ice water a tablespoon at a time until the dough just holds together. Pat into a disc, wrap and chill 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss the berries with sugar, cornstarch, lemon zest and juice.
  3. Roll the pastry into a rough 12-inch circle on parchment and slide it onto a baking sheet. Pile the berries in the centre, leaving a 2-inch border.
  4. Fold the border up over the fruit, pleating as you go. Brush the pastry with egg and sprinkle with coarse sugar.
  5. Bake 40–45 minutes, until the crust is deep golden and the juices bubble. Cool at least 20 minutes so the filling sets.
Kitchen noteFrozen berries work too. Don't thaw them; add 5–10 minutes to the bake.
